Delete Your Claimly Account

This page explains how to delete your Claimly account and the data linked to it, on iOS or Android.

Option 1: In the app (instant)

  1. Open Claimly and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and data are erased immediately.

Option 2: By email

If you no longer have the app or can’t sign in, email anannyashree1425@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject “Claimly account deletion request”. We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in email
  • All warranties and their details (products, prices, dates, stores, notes, coverage periods)
  • All receipt and document photos

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. We do not keep backups of deleted accounts. Feedback messages you sent separately are kept only as long as needed to respond to them, and are deleted on request.

Using Claimly without an account

If you never signed in, your data exists only on your phone. Remove it with Settings → Account → Delete Data on This Phone, or by uninstalling the app.
