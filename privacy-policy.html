Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy explains what information Claimly (“the app”, “we”, “us”) collects on iOS and Android, why, and the choices you have. Claimly is built and run by an independent developer based in New York, United States.

The short version

  • You can use Claimly without an account. Your warranties and receipt photos then stay only on your phone.
  • If you choose to sign in, your data is stored in your private account so it can sync and be backed up.
  • No ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and we never sell or share your data for advertising.
  • You can delete everything at any time from inside the app.

Information we collect

Without an account (Guest mode)

Everything you enter — product names, brands, prices, dates, stores, notes, coverage periods and receipt photos — is saved on your device only. We do not receive it. If you delete the app or use “Delete Data on This Phone”, it is gone.

With an account (optional)

InformationWhy
Email address and password (password is stored hashed, never readable by us)To create your account, sign you in, and send password-reset emails
Warranty details you enter (product, brand, model, store, price, dates, notes, coverage periods)To store, sync and back up your vault across your devices
Receipt and document photos you attachTo store them with the matching warranty. They are kept in private storage that only your account can access

When you use certain features

  • Camera and photo library — only when you choose to attach a receipt. We only access the photos you pick.
  • Notifications — expiry reminders (30 and 7 days before) are scheduled on your phone. No reminder data is sent to us.
  • Recall checks — to look for product recalls, the app sends the brand names from your vault (nothing else, no account information) to the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission’s public recall service at saferproducts.gov.
  • Import from Email — order emails you paste are read on your phone to find products. The text is not sent to us.
  • Feedback (“Talk to Me”) — if you send a message, we receive the message, the topic you choose, the app version and your phone’s operating system and version. Your email is included only if you type it in so we can reply.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, microphone audio, browsing history, advertising identifiers or payment information, and we do not use analytics or advertising SDKs.

How we use information

Only to run the app’s features described above: storing and syncing your vault, signing you in, showing reminders and recalls, and answering feedback. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell it.

Service providers

We rely on a small number of providers who process data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase — account sign-in, database and private file storage (only if you create an account).
  • Formspree — delivers feedback messages to our inbox (only if you send feedback).
  • U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission — public recall data (receives brand names only).
  • Apple / Google — app distribution and, if you choose, App Store / Play Store ratings.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of users or the public.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Account data is protected by row-level security, so each account can only read its own warranties and receipt photos. Receipt photos are shown using short-lived private links. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Guest data stays on your phone until you delete it (Settings → Account → Delete Data on This Phone) or uninstall the app.
  • Account data is kept while your account exists. Delete it any time in the app: Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently erases your account, all warranties and all receipt photos right away.
  • Can’t access the app? See how to request account deletion.
  • Feedback emails are kept only as long as needed to respond.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or California law), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, or object to its processing. You can do most of this directly in the app; for anything else, email us and we will respond within 30 days.

International users

Our providers may store data on servers outside your country. We only use providers that protect data in line with this policy.

Children

Claimly is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
