Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a warranty?
Tap the "+" button on the Vault screen, fill in the product details and warranty dates, and save. You can attach a receipt photo from your camera or photo library.

Do I need an account?
No — you can use Claimly in Guest Mode with data stored only on your device. Creating a free account syncs your warranties to the cloud and unlocks backup across devices.

How do I get expiry reminders?
Enable push notifications from the Reminders tab. Claimly sends alerts 30 days and 7 days before a warranty expires.

How do I delete my account and data?
Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, warranties, and receipt photos and cannot be undone.

How do I export a warranty as a PDF?
Open any item and tap "Export PDF" to generate a shareable PDF with the warranty details and receipt photo.
