Claimly Support

Claimly is a warranty and receipt tracking app for iOS and Android. This page covers how to get help, report a problem, or ask a question.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a warranty?
Tap the "+" button on the Vault screen, fill in the product details and warranty dates, and save. You can attach a receipt photo from your camera or photo library.

Do I need an account?
No. Claimly works fully without an account, and your data stays only on your phone. Signing in is optional: it backs up your warranties and syncs them across your devices, and anything you already added comes with you.

How do I get expiry reminders?
Go to Settings → Manage Reminders and turn them on. Claimly reminds you 30 days and 7 days before a warranty expires. On Android, make sure notifications for Claimly are allowed in your phone settings.

How do I delete my account and data?
Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, warranties, and receipt photos and cannot be undone. Can’t sign in? See how to request deletion by email.

How do I export a warranty as a PDF?
Open any item and tap "Export PDF" to generate a shareable PDF with the warranty details and receipt photo.
